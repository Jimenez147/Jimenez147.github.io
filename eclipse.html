Description




The app used to control the blinds has two modes of operation: direct control and motion scheduling. The app has two buttons to open or close the blinds immediately, and it also offers the option to schedule them to open or close at any time of day. It sends the instructions via Bluetooth to the microcontroller, which stores them in its internal memory to execute them at the desired time.


Motorized blackout blinds that cover the entire perimeter of the window to completely block out light, controlled by a microcontroller in a bain module connected to a power source, which communicates via Bluetooth with a mobile app to control their movement.
